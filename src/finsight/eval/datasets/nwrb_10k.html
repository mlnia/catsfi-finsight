PART I
Item 1. Business

Northwind Robotics Inc. designs, manufactures and sells autonomous mobile robots for warehouse logistics, together with Fleetmind, a subscription software platform that orchestrates robot fleets. The Company was founded in 2012 and is headquartered in Austin, Texas.

The Company reports two segments: Robotics Hardware and Fleetmind Software. Fleetmind Software revenue is recognized ratably over the subscription term, which is typically three years.

Item 1

As of December 31, 2025, the Company had approximately 4,200 full-time employees, of whom 1,650 work in research and development. The Company sells primarily through a direct sales force and a network of system-integrator partners in North America and Europe.

Competition in warehouse automation is intense. Principal competitors include large industrial automation vendors and venture-funded robotics start-ups. The Company competes on navigation accuracy, total cost of ownership and the breadth of the Fleetmind integration catalog.

Item 1A. Risk Factors

The Company depends on a single supplier, Kestrel Semiconductor, for the lidar sensors used in every robot. Any disruption at Kestrel Semiconductor could delay shipments and materially reduce net revenue.

A significant portion of components is manufactured in Taiwan and Malaysia. Tariffs, export controls or geopolitical tension in these regions could increase costs and interrupt the supply chain.

Customer concentration is high: the three largest customers accounted for 41% of total net revenue in fiscal 2025. The loss of any of these customers could adversely affect results of operations.

Item 1A

Security vulnerabilities in Fleetmind could allow unauthorized access to customer warehouse systems, resulting in liability, remediation costs and reputational harm.

PART II
Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis of Financial Condition and Results of Operations

Total net revenue was $2,418 million in fiscal 2025, an increase of 18% compared with $2,049 million in fiscal 2024, driven primarily by a 34% increase in Fleetmind Software subscriptions.

Gross margin improved to 46.5% in fiscal 2025 from 43.1% in fiscal 2024, reflecting the higher mix of software revenue and lower lidar sensor costs.

Research and development expense was $512 million in fiscal 2025 compared with $447 million in fiscal 2024, reflecting continued investment in navigation models and the Fleetmind platform.

Item 7

Net income was $286 million in fiscal 2025 compared with $198 million in fiscal 2024. Diluted earnings per share were $2.31 compared with $1.62 in the prior year.

As of December 31, 2025, cash and cash equivalents were $1,064 million. Management believes existing cash and operating cash flow will be sufficient to fund operations for at least the next twelve months.

For fiscal 2026, management expects Fleetmind Software to exceed 30% of total net revenue and plans capital expenditures of approximately $140 million, primarily for a new assembly facility in Monterrey, Mexico.
